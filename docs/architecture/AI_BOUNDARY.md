# AI Boundary

implementation_authority: NONE
current_runtime_status: DESIGN_ONLY

Future AI may summarize verified evidence, cluster detections, explain deterministic rules,
prioritize incidents, suggest defensive investigation, draft rules for review, and identify coverage
gaps. Inputs must be authorized, minimized, linked to evidence IDs, and labeled for telemetry quality.

AI must not override deterministic evidence, invent telemetry, infer universal safety, mutate active
rules, authorize destructive response, or receive raw content without case/role/privacy authority.
Every output is advisory, attributable to model/configuration version, and reversible by discarding it.
AI absence or failure cannot disable deterministic scanning, evidence production, or response policy.
